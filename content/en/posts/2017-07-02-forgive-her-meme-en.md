## Green Hat

The term "green hat" seems to have been around for a long time.

Some say in the Spring and Autumn period,
family members of people who made a living from prostitution had to wrap their heads in green cloth,
to distinguish noble from lowly.

There's also a funnier saying that
in ancient times there was a married lady who had an affair,
every morning when her husband went out,
she'd put a green hat on him,
as an obvious signal.

In any case,
no matter what,
this somewhat insulting term has been passed down.

But a recent meme has weakened the insulting quality of this term...

## Saint Patrick's Day

Ireland has a holiday called [Saint Patrick's Day][sp-day],
roughly about how a long long time ago,
a remarkable person came to Ireland
and spread Christianity to inspire everyone...

So on that day,
everyone wears clothes, socks, hats in the color of the Irish flag (green),
gathers together,
drinks...

Just like Chinese people,
no matter what holiday it is, it turns into Valentine's Day.
Many foreign holidays
ultimately turn into drinking holidays...

So what do we do about that?
Of course we choose to forgive her~
