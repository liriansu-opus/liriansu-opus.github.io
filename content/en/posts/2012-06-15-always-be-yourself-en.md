A long long time ago, in a candy store, there was a very beautiful beige candy.



When she was little she didn't know any better, her mom and dad both loved her dearly, her candy coating was the sweetest and most beautiful, and she skipped along by herself toward the display window.



She met a red candy and a green candy and one black candy.



The red candy said you shouldn't be beige, your candy coating should be a better-looking color.



The green candy said your beige is so pretty I love it, and then drew close to the beige candy and secretly rubbed her own color onto her.



The black candy said your beige is beautiful, you should be yourself.



There are many red candies, many green candies, and only one black candy.



The beige candy didn't know who to listen to, and this kind of thing can only be decided by oneself.



The beige candy decided to forget her own color and be good friends with everyone.



This story has no ending because the author can no longer find the beige candy.



Only the black candy was very sad.
